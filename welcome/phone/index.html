Use Broen on your phone

Open Broen in your browser, then add it to your home screen. It opens full screen like an app and works offline for studying. On your home screen it is called Dansk.

iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. Open Broen in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, the square with an arrow.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open Broen in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu.
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).

Computer (Chrome or Edge)

  1. Open Broen.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, then Install.

A native iPhone app is on its way.

Broen is Danish for “the bridge.” It is an independent app made by Isolated Routes.
