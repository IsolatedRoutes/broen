About Broen

Made by Isolated Routes. Open Broen in your browser, or add it to your phone.

Broen—meaning “The Bridge”—is a Danish learning app for curious people who learn best by tapping into the world around them.

It’s built on a deck of about 8,000 words and phrases that you can add to by uploading photos and text from the real Danish you encounter in your daily life.

Go deeper whenever you’re curious: The optional AI companion, which runs through your own Claude (Anthropic) or Gemini (Google) account, enables you to ask questions, deep dive on grammar, and make connections between words. Words and sentences can be read aloud in Danish.

You can upload a photo or paste Danish text and the AI assistant will translate it, analyze the sentence structure, and pick out key words that you can turn into cards you keep and review.

Broen lets you study by level, word type or topic, and words you know come back later in new forms, spaced over days, so you keep meeting them without drilling.

Broen is Danish for “the bridge.” It is an independent app made by Isolated Routes.
