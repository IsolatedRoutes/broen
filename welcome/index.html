Your bridge to learning Danish.

Broen is a Danish study app where you control what you want to learn. It has 8,000 flashcards you can sort by level, topic or word type, an AI tutor you can ask about any word or phrase, and a place to save the words you look up.

“Hvad” means “what” and asks about a thing: Hvad er det? (What is that?). For a person, use hvem: Hvem er det? (Who is that?). Said alone, Hvad? means “Pardon?”, but it can sound blunt, so Undskyld? (Excuse me?) is more polite.

When do I use “ikke” in a sentence?

The verb always comes second in a Danish sentence. If you start with “i dag”, the subject (jeg) moves behind the verb.

Jeg spiser i dag (I eat today)
I dag spiser jeg (Today I eat)

How it works

1

You decide what to study

Study only the level you want, from A1 to C2, or only one topic, like Food & Drink, or only verbs. There is no course to follow, and the 100 grammar lessons are optional.

2

Ask about any word

Type a question the way you would ask a teacher: why it is en bil but et hus, what a phrase really means, how to say something politely. Keep going with follow-up questions. The answers come from Claude or Gemini, through your own account.

3

Keep the words you look up

Normally a word you look up is gone as soon as you read the answer. In Broen you can photograph a sign, translate it, see why the sentence is built the way it is, and pick words from it. They come back later as flashcards.

Use on your phone

Open Broen in your browser, then add it to your home screen. It opens full screen like an app and works offline for studying. On your home screen it is called Dansk.

iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. Open Broen in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, the square with an arrow.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open Broen in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu.
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).

Computer (Chrome or Edge)

  1. Open Broen.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, then Install.

Common questions

What does it cost?

Nothing. There is no account and no ads. Only the AI part can cost money, because it runs on your own Claude or Gemini account. With Claude a question costs about half a cent. Gemini has a free tier.

Does it work offline?

For studying, yes. After you open Broen once, the flashcards, the dictionary, the grammar lessons and the notes on each word work without internet. Only the AI needs a connection.

What level is it for?

Mostly people past the beginner stage. The words cover every level from A1 to C2, so you can start lower if you need to.

Where is my progress saved?

In your browser, on your own device. Nothing is kept on our servers. To use a second device, turn on sync with Google Drive or Dropbox, or move a backup file across. Clearing your browser’s site data erases your progress, so keep a backup.

Is my information private?

Yes. There is no account, no ads and no tracking. The only thing that leaves your device is what you send to the AI, and only when you tap it. It goes to the company you chose, not through us. Here is the privacy policy.

Can I hear how a word sounds?

Yes. Tap the speaker next to any Danish word and your device reads it aloud. How good it sounds depends on the Danish voice installed on your device, and Broen shows you how to get a better one.
