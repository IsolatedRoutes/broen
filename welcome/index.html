Hvad hedder du? — What is your name?
Hvad laver du? — What are you doing?
“Hvad” means “what” and asks about a thing: Hvad er det? (What is that?). For a person, use hvem: Hvem er det? (Who is that?). Said alone, Hvad? means “Pardon?”, but it can sound blunt, so Undskyld? (Excuse me?) is more polite.

When do I use “ikke” in a sentence?

The verb always comes second in a Danish sentence. If you start with “i dag”, the subject (jeg) moves behind the verb.

Jeg spiser i dag (I eat today)
I dag spiser jeg (Today I eat)

word for word: gladden myself to

About Broen

Broen is Danish for “the bridge.” It is a Danish learning app for curious people who learn best from the world around them.

It starts with a deck of about 8,000 words and phrases, then grows with the real Danish you meet: a photo of a sign or a menu, a text from a friend, a sentence you want to understand. You keep the words you choose, and Broen brings them back when it helps.

Broen is an independent app made by Isolated Routes. Questions, ideas or corrections are welcome at isolatedroutes@gmail.com.

  • Free to use. No account, no sign-in and no payment to study.
  • No ads and no tracking. Nothing about you is collected.
  • Calm by design. No streaks, scores or timers.
  • Yours. Your cards and progress stay on your device, and you can back them up or sync them yourself.

Questions

Is Broen free? Do I need an account?

Broen is free, with no account and no sign-in. Your cards and progress stay on your device. Only the optional AI features can cost money, and only if you choose a paid provider.

How do I get Broen?

Open it in your browser from this page. There is nothing to download. To keep it on your home screen like an app, add it from your browser's menu (the steps are under Use Broen, above). A native iPhone app is on its way.

Does it work offline?

Yes, for studying. After you have opened Broen once, your cards, the dictionary and the grammar lessons work without internet. Only the AI features need a connection.

Do I need AI to use it?

No. Studying, the dictionary, grammar lessons and the explanations for the starter words all work without AI, and offline. AI powers the Assistant, photo reading and question features, and uses your own Claude or Gemini account.

What does AI cost?

The AI company bills you directly, only for what you use. With Claude, a question costs roughly half a cent and a photo a cent or two. Gemini has a free tier with daily limits. Prices are set by the companies and can change.

What level of Danish is it for?

It suits anyone past the very first steps, up to advanced learners. Words are sorted into four levels, and you choose which to study. It is not a course: you decide what to learn.

Can I use it on an iPad or a computer?

Yes. The website works in any current browser, on phones, tablets and computers. To keep two devices in step, turn on Sync in Backup and choose Google Drive or Dropbox.

Where is my progress saved? How do I move to a new device?

On your device, in the browser's own storage. Nothing is kept on our servers. To move to a new device, use Sync (Backup, then Sync) with Google Drive or Dropbox, or export a backup file and import it on the new device. Clearing the site's data in your browser erases your cards, so keep a backup.

Is my information private?

Yes. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics, and nothing is collected. Only text or photos you send to an AI feature leave your device, and only when you tap it. They go straight to the company you chose, never through us. Read the privacy policy.

Why are there no streaks, scores or timers?

So there is nothing to keep going and nothing to fail. If you miss a day, the app just picks up where you left off.

Can I hear how a word is pronounced?

Yes. Tap the speaker next to the Danish and your device reads it aloud. It normally works without internet. How natural it sounds depends on the Danish voice installed on your device, and Broen shows you how to get a better one.

What does “Broen” mean?

Broen is Danish for “the bridge.” The app is a bridge between the Danish around you and the Danish you know.

How do I contact you?

Write to isolatedroutes@gmail.com. Questions, ideas and corrections are all welcome.
