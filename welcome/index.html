“Hvad” means “what” and asks about a thing: Hvad er det? (What is that?). For a person, use hvem: Hvem er det? (Who is that?). Said alone, Hvad? means “Pardon?”, but it can sound blunt, so Undskyld? (Excuse me?) is more polite.

How it works

Broen is a powerful Danish learning tool that uses your natural curiosity about language and the world around you to build a deeper understanding.

Built on a deck of 8,000 words, phrases and grammar notes, Broen’s AI interface lets you dive deep into the areas that interest you, ask questions, and keep the words and concepts you come across for later review.

It takes the flashcard into the AI era, doesn’t force you into a fixed study program, and makes sure the Danish you actually come across becomes part of your learning.

Broen uses progressive learning while giving you the flexibility to shape your study around your needs, areas of focus and knowledge gaps.

The deck

8,000 words, phrases and grammar concepts that you can filter for focused study by word type (nouns, verbs, prepositions and more), topic (Food & Drink, Travel & Transport and more), level (from Basic, A1, up to Fluent, C2), or any combination of these. The deck uses a smart learning algorithm and spaced repetition to reinforce what you know.

Track progress

Star a card you want to focus on. Starred cards come up more often.

Check a card to mark it as known. Known cards are not shown again as themselves, but they are tracked so you can see your progress. They come back later in more advanced forms (for example, at gå (to go) later appears as gik (went) and er gået (has gone)) to move your learning forward.

Deepen understanding

Tap the lightbulb on any card to go deeper. It builds the connections that help you pick up the language naturally. For example, the lightbulb on dansk (Danish) shows the related word udansk (un-Danish), how to tell Danmark (Denmark) from dansk (the language) and en dansker (a Dane), and how other country words like svensk (Swedish) follow the same pattern.

Assistant

Take a photo of a sign, or paste in a news article or a message from a friend, in the Assistant tab. Broen translates it. Then you can analyze the sentence to understand how it is built and why, and to spot mistakes in Danish you wrote. Then use Extract text to pull out the key words and add them to your deck for later review.

This matters because it focuses you on the words you actually need, and they do not disappear from your memory.

Common questions

What does it cost?

Nothing. There is no account and no ads. Only the AI part can cost money, because it runs on your own Claude or Gemini account. With Claude a question costs about half a cent. Gemini has a free tier.

Does it work offline?

For studying, yes. After you open Broen once, the flashcards, the dictionary, the grammar lessons and the notes on each word work without internet. Only the AI needs a connection.

What level is it for?

Mostly people past the beginner stage. The words cover every level from A1 to C2, so you can start lower if you need to.

Where is my progress saved?

In your browser, on your own device. Nothing is kept on our servers. To use a second device, turn on sync with Google Drive or Dropbox, or move a backup file across. Clearing your browser’s site data erases your progress, so keep a backup.

Is my information private?

Yes. There is no account, no ads and no tracking. The only thing that leaves your device is what you send to the AI, and only when you tap it. It goes to the company you chose, not through us. Here is the privacy policy.

Can I hear how a word sounds?

Yes. Tap the speaker next to any Danish word and your device reads it aloud. How good it sounds depends on the Danish voice installed on your device, and Broen shows you how to get a better one.
