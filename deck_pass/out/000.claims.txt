afdød: "formal, polite word"; "min afdøde far"
akut: akutmodtagelse = "akut" + "modtagelse"; used as the term for an emergency room
ammunition: figurative use: "give ammunition" for facts in an argument
angående: "sounds formal"; "om" is the everyday word
anonym: "Anonyme Alkoholikere" is the Danish name for Alcoholics Anonymous
assistance: "more formal than hjælp"; shop worker may say "Kan jeg være til assistance?"
at afhænge af: "det kommer an på" as another way to say "it depends"
aske: "Askeonsdag" = Ash Wednesday (day that starts Lent)
antarktis: penguins live in Antarctica, polar bears in the Arctic
aluminium: Danish spelling matches British "aluminium"
april: "aprilsnar" = April fool
arbejdskraft: plural "arbejdskræfter" = the workers themselves ("udenlandske arbejdskræfter")
afgjort: "afgjort" before an adjective means "definitely"
arbejdstid: related words deltid, fuldtid, flextid
anger: "vrede" is the Danish word for anger
alvorlig: "Han er meget alvorlig" for a person who does not joke around
akademisk: "en akademiker" = person with a university degree
antibiotika: "antibiotikaresistens" = antibiotic resistance; used without an article
