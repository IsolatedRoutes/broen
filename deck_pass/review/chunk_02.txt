SENT_DA: Vi spiller fodbold på en ny **bane** ved skolen.
SENT_EN: We play soccer on a new field by the school.
RELATED: en tennisbane = a tennis court; en fodboldbane = a soccer field; en løbebane = a running track
## en banegård
EXPL: "En banegård" means "a railway station". It is built from "en bane" (a railway line) and "en gård" (a yard). To name the main station of a Danish city, "hoved" (main) goes in front: "Københavns Hovedbanegård" (Copenhagen Central Station).
SENT_DA: Vi venter på toget på en stor **banegård**.
SENT_EN: We wait for the train at a big railway station.
RELATED: et tog = a train; et spor = a platform / track; en togbillet = a train ticket
## en bank
EXPL: "En bank" means "a bank", as in the place for money. To say you are going to the bank, use "i" (in): "Jeg går i banken i dag" (I am going to the bank today).
SENT_DA: Jeg sætter mine penge ind i en **bank**.
SENT_EN: I put my money into a bank.
RELATED: et lån = a loan; en bankkonto = a bank account; en pengeautomat = an ATM
## en bankkonto
EXPL: "En bankkonto" means "a bank account". It is built from "en bank" (a bank) and "en konto" (an account). To say you open one, use "at oprette" (to set up): "Jeg vil gerne oprette en bankkonto" (I would like to open a bank account).
SENT_DA: Jeg har penge på min **bankkonto**.
SENT_EN: I have money in my bank account.
RELATED: et dankort = a Dankort; et kontonummer = an account number; en netbank = an online bank
## en bankrådgiver
EXPL: "En bankrådgiver" means "a bank adviser". It is built from "en bank" (a bank) and "en rådgiver" (an adviser). To say you are meeting one, use "et møde med" (a meeting with): "Jeg har et møde med min bankrådgiver" (I have a meeting with my bank adviser).
SENT_DA: Min **bankrådgiver** hjælper mig, når jeg skal låne penge.
SENT_EN: My bank adviser helps me when I need to borrow money.
RELATED: et lån = a loan; en bankfunktionær = a bank clerk; en investeringsrådgiver = an investment adviser
## en barbermaskine
EXPL: "En barbermaskine" means "an electric shaver". It is built from "at barbere" (to shave) and "en maskine" (a machine). For a razor you use by hand, say "en barberhøvl" (a razor): "Jeg køber en ny barberhøvl" (I am buying a new razor).
SENT_DA: Han barberer sig hver morgen med en **barbermaskine**.
SENT_EN: He shaves every morning with an electric shaver.
RELATED: barberskum = shaving foam; en skægtrimmer = a beard trimmer; et barberblad = a razor blade
## en barista
EXPL: "En barista" means "a barista". Do not mix it up with "en bartender" (a bartender). To talk about the person who makes coffee, say: "Baristaen laver en cappuccino" (The barista makes a cappuccino). To talk about the person who makes mixed drinks, say: "Bartenderen laver en cocktail" (The bartender makes a cocktail).
SENT_DA: En **barista** er uddannet til at lave god kaffe.
SENT_EN: A barista is trained to make good coffee.
RELATED: en kaffebar = a coffee bar; en café = a café; en kaffemaskine = a coffee machine; en tjener = a waiter
## en barndom
EXPL: "En barndom" means "a childhood". To say something happened when you were young, use "i" (in) before "min barndom" (my childhood): "I min barndom boede vi på landet" (In my childhood we lived in the country).
SENT_DA: Jeg tænker ofte på min **barndom** hos mormor.
SENT_EN: I often think of my childhood at Grandma's house.
RELATED: en ungdom = a youth; en alderdom = old age; en opvækst = an upbringing
## en barndomsven

SENT_DA: Han er min **barndomsven** fra børnehaven.
SENT_EN: He is my childhood friend from kindergarten.
RELATED: et venskab = a friendship; en kammerat = a buddy; en legekammerat = a playmate
## en barnedåb

SENT_EN: At a christening, the child gets its name in the church.
RELATED: en konfirmation = a confirmation; en dåbskjole = a christening gown; en gudmor = a godmother; en gudfar = a godfather
## en barnepige
EXPL: "En barnepige" means "a nanny or babysitter". It is built from "et barn" (a child) and "en pige" (a girl). To say you are the babysitter for someone, use "at være barnepige for" (to be a babysitter for): "Jeg er barnepige for min niece" (I am the babysitter for my niece).
SENT_DA: Vores **barnepige** passer børnene i aften.
SENT_EN: Our nanny is looking after the children tonight.
RELATED: en au pair = an au pair; en dagplejer = a home day-care provider; at passe børn = to look after children

## en bartender
EXPL: "En bartender" means "a bartender". Another word for the same job is "en barmand" (a barman): "Barmanden smiler til gæsterne" (The barman smiles at the guests).
SENT_DA: En **bartender** blander drinks og skænker øl i baren.
SENT_EN: A bartender mixes drinks and pours beer at the bar.
RELATED: en cocktail = a cocktail; en tjener = a waiter; en shaker = a shaker; en barista = a barista
## en base
EXPL: "En base" means "a base", as in a military base. In chemistry, it means a substance that reacts with an acid: "En base kan neutralisere en syre" (A base can neutralize an acid). For the bottom of an object, use "en bund" (a bottom): "Der er vand i bunden af flasken" (There is water in the bottom of the bottle).
SENT_DA: Soldaterne bor på en **base** tæt på grænsen.
SENT_EN: The soldiers live on a base close to the border.
RELATED: et hovedkvarter = a headquarters; en lejr = a camp; et fundament = a foundation
## en bedrager
EXPL: "En bedrager" means "a con artist". It comes from "at bedrage" (to deceive). To talk about the crime, use "bedrageri" (fraud): "Han blev dømt for bedrageri" (He was convicted of fraud).
SENT_DA: En **bedrager** lyver for at få andres penge.
SENT_EN: A con artist lies to get other people's money.
RELATED: en svindler = a swindler; en løgner = a liar; en falskner = a forger
## en bedrift
EXPL: "En bedrift" means "an achievement or feat". To say someone did something impressive, say: "Hans bedrift blev fejret af hele landet" (His achievement was celebrated by the whole country). When you talk about a business, it also means "business", as in "en landbrugsbedrift" (a farm business).
SENT_DA: At bestige Mount Everest uden ilt er en stor **bedrift**.
SENT_EN: Climbing Mount Everest without oxygen is a great feat.
RELATED: en præstation = an accomplishment; en dåd = a deed; en succes = a success; en rekord = a record
## en bedstefar
EXPL: "En bedstefar" means "a grandfather". To say he is your mother's father, use "morfar": "Min morfar er pensionist" (My mother's father is retired). To say he is your father's father, use "farfar": "Min farfar bor i Odense" (My father's father lives in Odense).
SENT_DA: Min **bedstefar** er min mors far.
SENT_EN: My grandfather is my mother's father.
RELATED: en bedstemor = a grandmother; et barnebarn = a grandchild; en oldefar = a great-grandfather
## en bedstemor
EXPL: "En bedstemor" means "a grandmother". To say she is your mother's mother, use "mormor": "Min mormor bager boller" (My mother's mother bakes buns). To say she is your father's mother, use "farmor": "Min farmor bor i Odense" (My father's mother lives in Odense).
SENT_DA: Min **bedstemor** er min fars mor.
SENT_EN: My grandmother is my father's mother.
RELATED: en bedstefar = a grandfather; et barnebarn = a grandchild; en oldemor = a great-grandmother

SENT_DA: Tandlægen gav mig en **bedøvelse**, så det ikke gjorde ondt.

## en befolkning
EXPL: "En befolkning" means "a population". To say the number of people is rising, use "at vokse" (to grow): "Befolkningen vokser hvert år" (The population grows every year). To talk about one person living in a town, use "en indbygger" (an inhabitant): "Byen har 50.000 indbyggere" (The town has 50,000 inhabitants).
SENT_DA: Danmarks **befolkning** er næsten seks millioner mennesker.
SENT_EN: Denmark's population is almost six million people.
RELATED: et folk = a nation; en borger = a citizen; et indbyggertal = a number of residents; en folketælling = a census
## en befrielse
EXPL: "En befrielse" means "a liberation". In Denmark, "Befrielsen" (the Liberation) is the name for the end of the German occupation in May 1945. When you feel a burden is gone, it also means "relief": "Det var en befrielse at aflevere opgaven" (It was a relief to hand in the assignment).
SENT_DA: Efter krigen fik landet sin **befrielse**, og folk dansede på gaden.
SENT_EN: After the war the country got its liberation, and people danced in the street.
RELATED: at befri = to free; en frihed = a freedom; en frigørelse = an emancipation; en besættelse = an occupation
