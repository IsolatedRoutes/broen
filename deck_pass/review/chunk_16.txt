## lige nu
EXPL: "Lige nu" means "right now". It is built from "lige" (just) and "nu" (now). To say something happens in a little while instead, use "om lidt" (in a little while): "Jeg kommer om lidt" (I am coming in a little while).
SENT_DA: Jeg har ikke tid **lige nu**.
SENT_EN: I do not have time right now.
RELATED: straks = immediately; senere = later; med det samme = right away
## lige om lidt
EXPL: "Lige om lidt" means "in just a moment". It is built from "om lidt" (in a little while) and "lige" (just).
SENT_DA: Maden er klar **lige om lidt**.
SENT_EN: The food will be ready in just a moment.
RELATED: om et øjeblik = in a moment; snart = soon; nu = now; for lidt siden = a little while ago
## ligefrem
EXPL: "Ligefrem" means "downright". To stress that something is very much so, put it before the describing word: "Det er ligefrem fantastisk" (It is downright fantastic). To say a person is direct and simple, use it for "straightforward": "en ligefrem mand" (a straightforward man).
SENT_DA: Det er **ligefrem** utroligt, at han vandt uden at træne.
SENT_EN: It is downright unbelievable that he won without training.
RELATED: direkte = direct; ærlig = honest; simpelthen = simply; ligeud = straight ahead
## ligeglad
EXPL: "Ligeglad" means "indifferent". To say you do not mind which choice is made, say: "Jeg er ligeglad" (I do not mind). To say someone does not care about other people, add "med" (about): "Han er ligeglad med andre" (He does not care about other people).
SENT_DA: Jeg er **ligeglad**, om vi spiser pizza eller pasta.
SENT_EN: I do not mind whether we eat pizza or pasta.
RELATED: ligegyldig = unimportant; interesseret = interested; nysgerrig = curious; at bekymre sig = to worry
## ligegyldig
EXPL: "Ligegyldig" means "unimportant". To say something does not matter, say: "Det er ligegyldigt" (It makes no difference). To say a person does not care, use it for the person: "Hun virker ligegyldig" (She seems indifferent).
SENT_DA: Han var **ligegyldig** og hørte ikke efter.
SENT_EN: He was indifferent and did not listen.
RELATED: ligeglad = indifferent; lige meget = no matter; uvigtig = unimportant; betydningsløs = meaningless
## ligeledes
EXPL: "Ligeledes" means "likewise". To answer a greeting with the same, reply: "Rart at møde dig!" "Ligeledes!" (Nice to meet you! Likewise!)
SENT_DA: Børn skal have tid til at lege, og voksne **ligeledes**.
SENT_EN: Children need time to play, and so do adults.
RELATED: i lige måde = same to you; ligeså = just as; desuden = besides; også = also
## ligesom
EXPL: "Ligesom" means "just like". It is built from "lige" (just) and "som" (like). To compare two people, put it before the second one: "Hun er høj ligesom sin mor" (She is tall just like her mother).
SENT_DA: Du er **ligesom** din far.
SENT_EN: You are just like your father.
RELATED: ens = identical; at ligne = to look like; på samme måde = in the same way; forskellig = different
## ligestilling
EXPL: "Ligestilling" means "equality". It is built from "lige" (equal) and "en stilling" (a position). To talk about equal treatment at work, say: "ligestilling på arbejdspladsen" (equality in the workplace).
SENT_DA: **Ligestilling** betyder, at mænd og kvinder har de samme muligheder.
SENT_EN: Equality means that men and women have the same opportunities.
RELATED: ligeværd = equal worth; en lighed = a similarity; diskrimination = discrimination; ligestillet = equal
## ligeså
EXPL: "Ligeså" means "just as". To say two things are equal, put it before the describing word: "Hun er ligeså høj som dig" (She is just as tall as you). You can also write it as two words with the same meaning: "lige så høj" (just as tall).
SENT_DA: Min bror er **ligeså** høj som far, men meget tyndere.
SENT_EN: My brother is just as tall as Dad, but much thinner.
RELATED: ligesom = just like; ligeledes = likewise; ens = identical; lignende = similar
## ligeud
EXPL: "Ligeud" means "straight ahead". To tell someone to go another way, use "til højre" (to the right) or "til venstre" (to the left): "Du skal til højre ved krydset" (You must go right at the intersection).
SENT_DA: Ved krydset skal du ikke dreje, men gå **ligeud**.
SENT_EN: At the intersection, do not turn, but go straight ahead.
RELATED: fremad = forward; tilbage = back; lige frem = straight ahead; at dreje = to turn
## ligeværdig
EXPL: "Ligeværdig" means "equal". It is built from "lige" (equal) and "værd" (worth). To say two sides count the same, say: "Vi er ligeværdige parter" (We are equal parties).
SENT_DA: I et godt forhold skal man føle sig **ligeværdig**.
SENT_EN: In a good relationship you must feel equal.
RELATED: ens = identical; ligestillet = equal in status; ligeberettiget = with equal rights; underlegen = inferior
## lignende
EXPL: "Lignende" means "similar". To say a problem is alike another one, put it before the noun: "et lignende problem" (a similar problem). To say one person looks like another, use "ligner" (looks like): "Han ligner sin far" (He looks like his father).
SENT_DA: Jeg har haft et **lignende** problem før.
SENT_EN: I have had a similar problem before.
RELATED: ens = identical; forskellig = different; samme = same; en lighed = a similarity
## lilla
EXPL: "Lilla" means "purple". To say a lighter shade, put "lys" (light) in front of it: "lyslilla" (light purple).
SENT_DA: Druer kan være grønne eller **lilla**.
SENT_EN: Grapes can be green or purple.
RELATED: blå = blue; rød = red; violet = violet; rosa = pink
## lille
EXPL: "Lille" means "small". To talk about more than one thing, use "små" (small), which looks different from "lille": "små biler" (small cars). To say someone is dear and little, say: "min lille ven" (my little friend).
SENT_DA: Musen er meget **lille**.
SENT_EN: The mouse is very small.
RELATED: en lillebror = a little brother; en lillesøster = a little sister; mindre = smaller; stor = big
## lim
EXPL: "Lim" means "glue". The verb is "at lime" (to glue): "Jeg limer billedet på papiret" (I glue the picture on the paper).
SENT_DA: **Lim** holder to stykker papir sammen.
SENT_EN: Glue holds two pieces of paper together.
RELATED: en limstift = a glue stick; at klistre = to stick; en limpistol = a glue gun; en tape = tape
## linser
EXPL: "Linser" means "lentils". To say one lentil, use "en linse": "Der ligger en linse på gulvet" (There is a lentil on the floor). The same word also means a lens: "en kontaktlinse" (a contact lens).
SENT_DA: Jeg laver suppe af røde **linser** og gulerødder.
SENT_EN: I make soup from red lentils and carrots.
RELATED: kikærter = chickpeas; bønner = beans; ærter = peas
## litteratur
EXPL: "Litteratur" means "literature". To say all that has been written about a subject, add "om" (about): "litteraturen om søvn" (the literature on sleep).
SENT_DA: **Litteratur** er for eksempel romaner, digte og skuespil.
SENT_EN: Literature is, for example, novels, poems and plays.
RELATED: en forfatter = an author; en bog = a book; en digter = a poet; et bibliotek = a library
## livlig
EXPL: "Livlig" means "lively". Do not mix it up with "levende" (alive): "et levende dyr" (a living animal) is not dead, while "en livlig fest" (a lively party) is full of energy.
SENT_DA: Gaden er **livlig** om aftenen, fordi mange restauranter har åbent.
SENT_EN: The street is lively in the evening because many restaurants are open.
RELATED: energisk = energetic; stille = quiet; rolig = calm; travl = busy
## livsglæde
EXPL: "En livsglæde" means "joy of life". It is built from "et liv" (a life) and "en glæde" (a joy). To say a person has it, use "livsglad" (full of joy of life): "Hun er livsglad" (She is full of joy of life).
SENT_DA: Selv som gammel mand havde han en stor **livsglæde**.
SENT_EN: Even as an old man he had a great zest for life.
RELATED: en lykke = happiness; optimisme = optimism; begejstring = enthusiasm; munterhed = cheerfulness
## livskvalitet
EXPL: "Livskvalitet" means "quality of life". It is built from "et liv" (a life) and "en kvalitet" (a quality). To say a hobby makes your life better, say: "Hobbyen giver mig livskvalitet" (The hobby gives me quality of life).
SENT_DA: Fritid giver mig **livskvalitet**, fordi jeg kan være sammen med familien.
SENT_EN: Free time gives me quality of life because I can be with my family.
RELATED: velvære = well-being; en livsstil = a lifestyle; sundhed = health; tryghed = security
## logik
EXPL: "Logik" means "logic". To say there is a reason behind something, say: "Der er en logik i det" (There is logic in it). The describing word is "logisk" (logical).
SENT_DA: Matematik bygger på **logik**, og hvert trin følger af det forrige.
SENT_EN: Math is built on logic, and each step follows from the previous one.
RELATED: et argument = an argument; en konklusion = a conclusion; fornuft = reason
## logisk
EXPL: "Logisk" means "logical". It comes from "en logik" (logic). To say something makes sense, say: "Det er helt logisk" (That is completely logical).
SENT_DA: Det er **logisk**, fordi to plus to er fire.
SENT_EN: It is logical, because two plus two is four.
RELATED: fornuftig = sensible; rationel = rational; ulogisk = illogical; konsekvent = consistent
## lokal
EXPL: "Lokal" means "local". To talk about news from your own town, say: "lokale nyheder" (local news).
SENT_DA: Jeg køber mad hos en **lokal** bager.
SENT_EN: I buy food from a local baker.
RELATED: global = global; regional = regional; national = national; international = international
## lokalt
EXPL: "Lokalt" means "locally". It comes from "lokal" (local). To say you buy things in your own area, put it after the verb: "Jeg handler lokalt" (I shop locally).
SENT_DA: Vi handler **lokalt**, fordi vi vil støtte byens bønder.
SENT_EN: We shop locally because we want to support the town's farmers.
RELATED: regionalt = regionally; nationalt = nationally; globalt = globally; en lokalitet = a location
## lommepenge
EXPL: "Lommepenge" means "pocket money". It is built from "en lomme" (a pocket) and "penge" (money). The word "lomme" is also in "en lommelygte" (a flashlight): "Jeg har en lommelygte i tasken" (I have a flashlight in my bag).
SENT_DA: Min datter får hundrede kroner i **lommepenge**.
SENT_EN: My daughter gets a hundred kroner in pocket money.
RELATED: småpenge = small change; ugepenge = weekly allowance; kontanter = cash
## lotto
EXPL: "Lotto" means "lotto". In Denmark, "Lotto" is run by the company "Danske Spil".
SENT_DA: Han håber at vinde i **lotto** i weekenden.
SENT_EN: He hopes to win at lotto this weekend.
RELATED: et lod = a lottery ticket; en gevinst = a prize; et lotteri = a lottery; at tabe = to lose
## lovende
EXPL: "Lovende" means "promising". It comes from the verb "at love" (to promise). To say a person has talent, say: "Hun er et lovende talent" (She is a promising talent).
SENT_DA: De første resultater af forsøget ser **lovende** ud.
SENT_EN: The first results of the experiment look promising.
RELATED: håbefuld = hopeful; opmuntrende = encouraging; talentfuld = talented; et løfte = a promise
## lovgivning
EXPL: "Lovgivning" means "legislation". This word has no article, so leave out "en": "Dansk lovgivning gælder for alle" (Danish legislation applies to everyone). It is built from "en lov" (a law) and "at give" (to give).
SENT_DA: Ny **lovgivning** skal sikre, at alle børn kan komme i skole.
SENT_EN: New legislation must ensure that all children can go to school.
RELATED: en forordning = a regulation; et lovforslag = a bill; en bekendtgørelse = an executive order; en lovgiver = a legislator
## lovlig
EXPL: "Lovlig" means "legal". To say the opposite, put "u" in front: "ulovlig" (illegal). To say stealing is not allowed, say: "Det er ulovligt at stjæle" (It is illegal to steal).
SENT_DA: Det er en **lovlig** handling, fordi loven tillader det.
SENT_EN: It is a legal act, because the law allows it.
RELATED: en lov = a law; tilladt = allowed; forbudt = forbidden; lovgivning = legislation
## lovpligtig
EXPL: "Lovpligtig" means "legally required". It is built from "en lov" (a law) and "pligtig" (obliged). To say a thing is required by law, say: "Cykellygter er lovpligtige" (Bike lights are required by law).
SENT_DA: Alle bilister skal have en forsikring, fordi den er **lovpligtig**.
SENT_EN: All drivers must have insurance because it is required by law.
RELATED: obligatorisk = mandatory; frivillig = voluntary; påkrævet = required; tvungen = forced
