## et stedord
EXPL: "Et stedord" means "a pronoun". It is built from "et sted" (a place) and "et ord" (a word), so it is literally a place word.
SENT_DA: Et **stedord** som han eller hun står i stedet for et navn.
SENT_EN: A pronoun like he or she stands in place of a name.
RELATED: et navneord = a noun; et tillægsord = an adjective; et udsagnsord = a verb

SENT_EN: Put your plug in the wall and the lamp works.
RELATED: en stikkontakt = an outlet; en ledning = a cord; en forlænger = an extension cord; en oplader = a charger
