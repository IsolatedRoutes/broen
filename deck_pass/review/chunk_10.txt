## et gear
EXPL: "Et gear" means "a gear". To say you move to a lower gear on a hill, say: "Jeg skifter ned" (I shift down). To move to a higher gear, say "skifte op" (shift up).
SENT_DA: Jeg skifter **gear**, når vejen går opad.
SENT_EN: I shift gears when the road goes uphill.
RELATED: en speeder = an accelerator; en bremse = a brake; en kobling = a clutch

SENT_DA: Banken tager et **gebyr** på halvtreds kroner for hver overførsel.
SENT_EN: The bank charges a fee of fifty kroner for each transfer.
RELATED: en afgift = a charge; et honorar = a professional fee; en takst = a rate; en bøde = a fine

SENT_DA: Jeg hjælper dig ikke. **Gør det selv**!
SENT_EN: I will not help you. Do it yourself!
RELATED: at gøre = to do; at lave = to make / do; at hjælpe = to help
## at hacke
EXPL: "At hacke" means "to hack", as in breaking into a computer. Do not mix it up with "at hakke" (to chop), which has two k: "Jeg hakker løg" (I chop onions).
SENT_DA: Nogen forsøgte at **hacke** firmaets netværk i går nat.
SENT_EN: Someone tried to hack the company's network last night.
RELATED: en hacker = a hacker; et hackerangreb = a cyberattack; en virus = a computer virus

SENT_DA: Du skal **holde fast**, når bussen kører.
SENT_EN: You must hold on when the bus is moving.
RELATED: at gribe = to grab; at slippe = to let go; at klamre sig til = to cling to
