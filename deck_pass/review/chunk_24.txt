## at strejke
EXPL: "At strejke" means "to strike", as in stopping work as a protest. To say the teachers are on strike, say: "Lærerne strejker" (The teachers are on strike). The noun is "strejke" (strike): "Der er strejke på skolen i dag" (There is a strike at the school today).
SENT_DA: Lærerne vil **strejke**, fordi de ønsker højere løn.
SENT_EN: The teachers want to strike because they want higher pay.
RELATED: en fagforening = a union; en overenskomst = a collective agreement; at demonstrere = to demonstrate; en arbejdsgiver = an employer

SENT_DA: En **sodavand** er sød og har bobler.
SENT_EN: A soda is sweet and has bubbles.
RELATED: en drik = a drink; en limonade = a lemonade; en juice = a juice
## en sodavandsis
EXPL: "En sodavandsis" means "a popsicle". It is built from "en sodavand" (a soda) and "en is" (an ice), so literally it is a soda ice.
SENT_DA: En **sodavandsis** er frossen sodavand på en pind.
SENT_EN: A popsicle is frozen soda on a stick.
RELATED: en isvaffel = an ice cream cone; en softice = a soft serve; en dessert = a dessert
## en sofa
EXPL: "En sofa" means "a sofa". The low table in front of it is "et sofabord" (a coffee table), built from "sofa" and "et bord" (a table): "Kopperne står på sofabordet" (The cups are on the coffee table).
SENT_DA: Vi sidder på en blød **sofa**.
SENT_EN: We sit on a soft sofa.
RELATED: en lænestol = an armchair; en sofapude = a sofa cushion; en sovesofa = a sofa bed
## en sok
EXPL: "En sok" means "a sock". To talk about a pair, use "et par sokker" (a pair of socks): "Jeg har købt et par sokker" (I bought a pair of socks).
SENT_DA: Jeg har en **sok** på min venstre fod.
SENT_EN: I have a sock on my left foot.
RELATED: en strømpe = a stocking / sock; en sko = a shoe; en handske = a glove
## en sol
EXPL: "En sol" means "a sun". To say the sun is shining, use the form with "-en": "Solen skinner" (The sun is shining). The word "sol" is also the start of "solskin" (sunshine) and "solbriller" (sunglasses).
SENT_DA: Der er meget **sol** i dag.
SENT_EN: There is a lot of sun today.
RELATED: en måne = a moon; en stjerne = a star; en solopgang = a sunrise; en solnedgang = a sunset
## en solcelle
EXPL: "En solcelle" means "a solar cell". It is built from "sol" (sun) and "en celle" (a cell). Many cells together form "et solcelleanlæg" (a solar cell system): "Vi har et solcelleanlæg på taget" (We have a solar cell system on the roof).
SENT_DA: Hver **solcelle** laver sollys om til strøm.
SENT_EN: Each solar cell turns sunlight into electricity.
RELATED: et solpanel = a solar panel; en vindmølle = a wind turbine; en varmepumpe = a heat pump; et batteri = a battery
## en soldat

SENT_EN: A soldier wears a uniform and fights for his country.
RELATED: en hær = an army; en officer = an officer; en kriger = a warrior

## en solnedgang
EXPL: "En solnedgang" means "a sunset". It is built from "sol" (sun) and "en nedgang" (a going down). The part "nedgang" also means a drop in numbers: "en nedgang i priserne" (a drop in prices).
SENT_DA: Vi ser en **solnedgang** fra stranden.
SENT_EN: We watch a sunset from the beach.
RELATED: en solopgang = sunrise; en solstråle = sunbeam; et tusmørke = twilight; en horisont = horizon
## en solopgang
EXPL: "En solopgang" means "a sunrise". It is built from "sol" (sun) and "en opgang" (a going up). The part "opgang" is also the stairwell entrance of an apartment building: "Vi bor i opgang 3" (We live in entrance 3).
SENT_DA: Vi står tidligt op og ser en **solopgang**.
SENT_EN: We get up early and watch a sunrise.
RELATED: en solnedgang = sunset; et daggry = dawn; en morgen = morning

## en sommer
EXPL: "En sommer" means "a summer". To say what is true in the summer in general, use "om sommeren": "Om sommeren er det lyst i lang tid" (In the summer it is light for a long time).
SENT_DA: I **sommer** bader vi i havet.
SENT_EN: This summer we swim in the sea.
RELATED: en vinter = a winter; et forår = a spring; et efterår = an autumn

## en sommernat
EXPL: "En sommernat" means "a summer night". The plural changes the vowel: "to sommernætter" (two summer nights). In Denmark summer nights are short and light, so you can say "Det er stadig lyst klokken ti" (It is still light at ten o'clock).
SENT_DA: Vi sover med åbent vindue på en varm **sommernat**.
SENT_EN: We sleep with the window open on a warm summer night.
RELATED: en vinternat = a winter night; en sommerdag = a summer day; en sommeraften = a summer evening
