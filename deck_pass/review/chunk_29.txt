SENT_DA: En **uge** har syv dage.
SENT_EN: A week has seven days.
RELATED: en dag = a day; en måned = a month; et år = a year; en weekend = a weekend
## en ugeavis
EXPL: "En ugeavis" means "a weekly local paper". It is built from "en uge" (a week) and "en avis" (a newspaper). To say where you read something, add "i" (in): "Det stod i ugeavisen" (It was in the weekly paper).
SENT_DA: Vi får en **ugeavis** i postkassen hver uge.
SENT_EN: We get a weekly local paper in the mailbox every week.
RELATED: et dagblad = a daily paper; en lokalavis = a local paper; et blad = a magazine
## en ugedag
EXPL: "En ugedag" means "a day of the week". It is built from "uge" (week) and "dag" (day). Inside a sentence, a day name is written without a capital, unlike in English: "mandag" (Monday).
SENT_DA: Mandag er en **ugedag**.
SENT_EN: Monday is a day of the week.
RELATED: en weekend = a weekend; en hverdag = a weekday; tirsdag = Tuesday; søndag = Sunday
