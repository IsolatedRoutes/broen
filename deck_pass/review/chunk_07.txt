## at føre
EXPL: "At føre" means "to lead or conduct". To say someone runs a conversation: "Han fører samtalen" (He is leading the conversation). To say you keep accounts or records: "Vi fører regnskab" (We keep the accounts).
SENT_DA: Vejen kan **føre** dig til stranden.
SENT_EN: The road can lead you to the beach.
RELATED: at lede = to lead; at styre = to steer; at vise vej = to show the way; at bringe = to bring

SENT_DA: Jeg køber frisk laks hos en **fiskehandler**.
SENT_EN: I buy fresh salmon from a fishmonger.
RELATED: en ostehandler = a cheese seller; en slagter = a butcher; en bager = a baker; en grønthandler = a greengrocer
## en fisker
EXPL: "En fisker" means "a fisherman". To say what a fisherman does, use "at fange" (to catch): "Fiskeren fanger torsk" (The fisherman catches cod). Do not mix it up with "en fiskehandler" (a fishmonger): "Fiskehandleren sælger frisk laks" (The fishmonger sells fresh salmon).
SENT_DA: Vores nabo er **fisker** og sejler hver morgen.
SENT_EN: Our neighbor is a fisherman and sails every morning.
RELATED: en fiskekutter = a fishing boat; et fiskenet = a fishing net; en fiskerby = a fishing village
