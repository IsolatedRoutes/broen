## en begyndelse
EXPL: "En begyndelse" means "a beginning". It is built from "at begynde" (to begin). To say it happens in the first part of something, use "i begyndelsen af" (at the beginning of): "I begyndelsen af filmen er der en storm" (At the beginning of the film there is a storm).
SENT_DA: Et eventyr har en **begyndelse** og en slutning.
SENT_EN: A fairy tale has a beginning and an ending.
RELATED: en slutning = an ending; en start = a start; en afslutning = a conclusion; en ende = an end
## en begynder
EXPL: "En begynder" means "a beginner". It comes from "at begynde" (to begin). To talk about a class for beginners, use "et begynderhold" (a beginners' class): "Jeg går på et begynderhold" (I am in a beginners' class).
SENT_DA: Jeg er kun **begynder**, men jeg lærer hurtigt.
SENT_EN: I am only a beginner, but I learn fast.
RELATED: en nybegynder = a novice; en ekspert = an expert; en amatør = an amateur; en professionel = a professional

## en bekendt
EXPL: "En bekendt" means "an acquaintance". To say someone is not a close friend, say: "Han er en bekendt, ikke en nær ven" (He is an acquaintance, not a close friend). When a song or a face seems known to you, the adjective "bekendt" means "familiar": "Sangen lyder bekendt" (The song sounds familiar).
SENT_DA: Jeg mødte en **bekendt** i byen.
SENT_EN: I met an acquaintance in town.
RELATED: at kende = to know; kendt = famous; ukendt = unknown
