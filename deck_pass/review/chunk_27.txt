## stille
EXPL: "Stille" means "quiet". To tell someone to be quiet, say: "Vær stille" (Be quiet). For a relaxed person, use "rolig" (calm): "Hun er meget rolig" (She is very calm). The verb "at stille" (to put) is a different use: "at stille et spørgsmål" (to ask a question).
SENT_DA: Det er meget **stille** i huset.
SENT_EN: It is very quiet in the house.
RELATED: høj = loud; stilhed = silence; tavs = silent; støjende = noisy
## stillehavet
EXPL: "Stillehavet" means "the Pacific Ocean". It is built from "stille" (calm) and "hav" (ocean), with "-et" at the end for "the". To say where a country lies, say: "Japan ligger ved Stillehavet" (Japan lies by the Pacific).
SENT_DA: **Stillehavet** er det største hav på Jorden og ligger mellem Asien og Amerika.
SENT_EN: The Pacific Ocean is the biggest ocean on Earth and lies between Asia and America.
RELATED: Atlanterhavet = the Atlantic; Nordsøen = the North Sea; Middelhavet = the Mediterranean; Ishavet = the Arctic Ocean
## stinkende

RELATED: en stank = a stench; ildelugtende = foul-smelling; duftende = fragrant

## stolt af
EXPL: "Stolt af" means "proud of". To name the person you are proud of, put the name or "dig" (you) after "af": "Jeg er stolt af dig" (I am proud of you). To name an achievement you are proud of, put "at" (to) and a verb after "af": "stolt af at have klaret det" (proud of having managed it).

To say you dry plates, say: "Hun tørrer tallerkenerne af" (She dries the plates). To mop up a spill, use "at tørre op" (to wipe up): "Jeg tørrer vandet op" (I wipe up the water).
SENT_DA: Jeg skal **tørre af** efter middagen.
SENT_EN: I have to dry the dishes after dinner.
RELATED: at vaske op = to do the dishes; en karklud = a dishcloth; at gøre rent = to clean; et viskestykke = a dish towel
## at tørre støv af
EXPL: "At tørre støv af" means "to dust". It is built from "at tørre" (to wipe), "støv" (dust) and "af" (off). To say how often someone dusts, put "af" (off) at the end: "Han tørrer støv af hver uge" (He dusts every week).
SENT_DA: Jeg skal **tørre støv af** på hylderne i dag.
SENT_EN: I have to dust the shelves today.
RELATED: en klud = a cloth; en fjerkost = a feather duster; at feje = to sweep; at støvsuge = to vacuum
## en t-shirt
EXPL: "En t-shirt" means "a t-shirt". To say you wear one, use "at have" (to have) with "på" (on): "Jeg har en t-shirt på" (I am wearing a t-shirt). Literally, it is "I have a t-shirt on".
SENT_DA: Om sommeren går jeg i **t-shirt** og shorts.
SENT_EN: In the summer I wear a t-shirt and shorts.
RELATED: en skjorte = a shirt; en trøje = a sweater; en bluse = a blouse

SENT_EN: The team's tactic is to defend and wait for a chance.

SENT_DA: Man skal ikke tage hver **talemåde** helt bogstaveligt.
SENT_EN: You should not take every saying completely literally.
RELATED: et ordsprog = a proverb; et udtryk = an expression; et mundheld = a catchphrase; en vending = a turn of phrase

## en tand

SENT_DA: Jeg har ondt i en **tand** i dag.
SENT_EN: One of my teeth hurts today.
RELATED: en tandlæge = a dentist; en kindtand = a molar; en visdomstand = a wisdom tooth; tandpasta = toothpaste
