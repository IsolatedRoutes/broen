## udenfor
EXPL: "Udenfor" means "outside". To say where someone is: "Hunden sover udenfor" (The dog sleeps outside). To say where someone goes: "Vi går udenfor" (We go outside).
SENT_DA: Børnene leger **udenfor** i haven.
SENT_EN: The children play outside in the garden.
RELATED: indenfor = inside; ude = out / outside; ud = out (movement)
## udenlands
EXPL: "Udenlands" means "abroad" and names where you are or where you go: "Hun bor udenlands" (She lives abroad). Do not mix it up with "udenlandsk" (foreign), which describes a thing: "en udenlandsk bil" (a foreign car).
SENT_DA: Hun har studeret **udenlands** i to år.
SENT_EN: She has studied abroad for two years.
RELATED: i udlandet = abroad; hjemme = at home; en udlænding = a foreigner; en udlandsrejse = a trip abroad
## udenlandsk

RELATED: indenlandsk = domestic; udlandet = abroad; fremmed = foreign / strange; en udlænding = a foreigner

## udkig
EXPL: "Et udkig" means "lookout", as in a high place to look from. To say you are searching for something, use "på udkig efter" (on the lookout for): "Jeg er på udkig efter en lejlighed" (I am on the lookout for an apartment). Do not mix it up with "en udsigt" (a view), which is what you see: "Der er en flot udsigt" (There is a nice view).
SENT_DA: Fra det høje **udkig** kunne vi se hele byen.
SENT_EN: From the high lookout we could see the whole city.
RELATED: at spejde = to scout; at holde øje med = to keep an eye on; en vagt = a guard

SENT_DA: Han har svært ved at **vedkende sig** sine fejl.
SENT_EN: He finds it hard to acknowledge his mistakes.
RELATED: at indrømme = to admit; at påtage sig = to take on; at nægte = to deny
