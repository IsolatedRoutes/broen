## at krydse
EXPL: "At krydse" means "to cross". It comes from "et kryds" (a cross). To say you pass over a border: "Vi krydser grænsen i morgen" (We cross the border tomorrow).
SENT_DA: Børnene skal **krydse** gaden, når lyset er grønt.
SENT_EN: The children must cross the street when the light is green.
RELATED: en krydsning = a crossing; at gå over = to walk across; et vejkryds = an intersection
## at kræve
EXPL: "At kræve" means "to require". To say what a task needs: "Opgaven kræver tid" (The task requires time). A different use is "to demand", when you insist on something: "Han kræver svar" (He demands an answer).
SENT_DA: Det nye job vil **kræve** meget tid.
SENT_EN: The new job will require a lot of time.
RELATED: et krav = a demand / requirement; at forlange = to demand; at behøve = to need
## at kunne
EXPL: "At kunne" means "to be able to". To say you are able to do something, put the verb right after "kan", with no "at" (to): "Jeg kan svømme" (I can swim). To ask someone for help: "Kan du hjælpe mig?" (Can you help me?)
SENT_DA: Jeg vil gerne **kunne** svømme som en fisk.
SENT_EN: I would like to be able to swim like a fish.
RELATED: at ville = to want to / will; at skulle = to have to / should; en evne = an ability; at turde = to dare
## at kunne lide

SENT_DA: Du vil **kunne lide** maden her.
SENT_EN: You will like the food here.
RELATED: at elske = to love; at hade = to hate; at holde af = to be fond of; at synes om = to like

SENT_DA: Der bor otte milliarder mennesker på vores **klode**.
SENT_EN: Eight billion people live on our planet.
RELATED: en globus = a globe; en verden = a world; en måne = a moon; en sol = a sun

SENT_DA: På museet fortæller en **kunsthistoriker** om malerierne.
SENT_EN: At the museum an art historian tells about the paintings.
RELATED: en kunstner = an artist; en kurator = a curator; en arkæolog = an archaeologist; et kunstværk = an artwork
## en kunstner
EXPL: "En kunstner" means "an artist". It is built from "kunst" (art). To talk about a musician or singer, you also use it: "Han er en kendt kunstner, der synger" (He is a famous artist who sings).
SENT_DA: Den danske **kunstner** maler store billeder.
SENT_EN: The Danish artist paints big pictures.
RELATED: en maler = a painter; en billedhugger = a sculptor; kunstnerisk = artistic
## en kunstudstilling
EXPL: "En kunstudstilling" means "an art exhibition". It is built from "kunst" (art) and "en udstilling" (an exhibition). The opening evening is called "en fernisering" (an opening reception): "Vi er inviteret til ferniseringen" (We are invited to the opening).
SENT_DA: På en **kunstudstilling** kan man se malerier og skulpturer.
SENT_EN: At an art exhibition you can see paintings and sculptures.
RELATED: et galleri = a gallery; et kunstmuseum = an art museum; en kurator = a curator; en kunstner = an artist
## en kur
EXPL: "En kur" means "a cure". To talk about a fixed program of eating less to lose weight, use "en slankekur" (a weight-loss diet): "Hun er på slankekur" (She is on a diet).
SENT_DA: Lægerne leder efter en **kur** mod sygdommen.
SENT_EN: The doctors are looking for a cure for the disease.
RELATED: en behandling = a treatment; en medicin = a medicine; en helbredelse = a healing; et middel = a remedy
## en kurs
EXPL: "En kurs" means "a course (direction) or exchange rate". To say a pilot changes direction, use "at skifte kurs" (to change course): "Piloten skifter kurs" (The pilot changes course). To talk about money, use it for the exchange rate: "Dollarens kurs er høj i dag" (The dollar's exchange rate is high today). A class you attend is "et kursus" (a course): "Jeg tager et kursus i dansk" (I am taking a course in Danish).
SENT_DA: Skibet holder **kurs** mod nord, selvom bølgerne er store.
SENT_EN: The ship keeps its course north, even though the waves are big.
RELATED: et kompas = a compass; en valuta = a currency; en aktiekurs = a share price
## en kurv

SENT_DA: Æblerne ligger i en **kurv** med hank.
SENT_EN: The apples are in a basket with a handle.
RELATED: en papirkurv = a wastebasket; en cykelkurv = a bike basket; en brødkurv = a bread basket

## en kvalitet
EXPL: "En kvalitet" means "a quality". When you talk about how good something is, use it: "Det er god kvalitet" (It is good quality). When you talk about a good trait in a person, it also works: "Ærlighed er en kvalitet" (Honesty is a quality).
SENT_DA: Jeg vil hellere købe én ting af høj **kvalitet**.
SENT_EN: I would rather buy one thing of high quality.
RELATED: en egenskab = a trait; en standard = a standard; et niveau = a level

## en kø
EXPL: "En kø" means "a queue". To say you wait in line, use "at stå i kø" (to stand in a queue), with no "en": "Vi står i kø til biografen" (We are standing in line for the cinema). When you talk about cars, it also means a traffic jam: "Der er kø på motorvejen" (There is a traffic jam on the highway).
SENT_DA: Der er en lang **kø** ved kassen.
SENT_EN: There is a long line at the checkout.
RELATED: en ko = a cow; en ventetid = a wait time; en trafikprop = a traffic jam; at vente = to wait
