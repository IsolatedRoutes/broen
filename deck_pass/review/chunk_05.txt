SENT_EN: The birds start to sing at dawn, before the sun rises.
RELATED: en morgen = a morning; en solnedgang = a sunset; en skumring = dusk

SENT_DA: **Efterhånden som** børnene vokser, får de flere pligter.
SENT_EN: As the children grow, they get more duties.
RELATED: i takt med = in step with; gradvist = gradually; mere og mere = more and more
