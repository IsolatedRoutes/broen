## et fad
EXPL: "Et fad" means "a dish", as in a serving dish for the table. To say what is on the table, use it for the serving dish: "Der står et fad med kartofler på bordet" (There is a dish of potatoes on the table). Do not mix it up with "en tallerken" (a plate), which you eat from: "Jeg lægger maden på tallerkenen" (I put the food on the plate).
SENT_DA: Hun lægger kødet på et **fad**.
SENT_EN: She puts the meat on a dish.
RELATED: en skål = a bowl; en bakke = a tray; en gryde = a pot
