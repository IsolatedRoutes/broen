## loyal
EXPL: "Loyal" means "loyal". To say who you are loyal to, add "over for" (towards): "Han er loyal over for sin chef" (He is loyal to his boss).
SENT_DA: Han var **loyal** over for firmaet, selv da andre sagde op.
SENT_EN: He was loyal to the company, even when others quit.
RELATED: loyalitet = loyalty; trofast = faithful; pålidelig = reliable; illoyal = disloyal
