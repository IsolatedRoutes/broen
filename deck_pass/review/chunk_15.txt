## at like
EXPL: "At like" means "to like (online)". To say she likes a post, say: "Hun liker alle mine opslag" (She likes all my posts). For a person or food, use "at kunne lide" (to like): "Jeg kan lide pizza" (I like pizza).
SENT_DA: Jeg vil **like** dit billede på Instagram.
SENT_EN: I want to like your picture on Instagram.
RELATED: at synes om = to like, to approve of; at dele = to share; at følge = to follow

## at love

## at lovgive

## at lyde
EXPL: "At lyde" means "to sound". To say how an idea or plan seems to you, add an adjective: "Det lyder godt" (That sounds good). Do not mix it up with "at høre" (to hear), which is for noticing a noise: "Jeg hører musik" (I hear music).
SENT_DA: Det kan **lyde** som en god idé.
