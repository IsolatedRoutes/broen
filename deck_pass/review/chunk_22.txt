## på den ene side
EXPL: "På den ene side" means "on one hand". To start the first of two views, say: "På den ene side vil jeg gerne rejse, men jeg har ikke penge" (On one hand I would like to travel, but I have no money). To say where something is, it also means "on one side": "Haven ligger på den ene side af huset" (The garden is on one side of the house).
SENT_DA: **På den ene side** vil jeg rejse, men jeg er også bange.
SENT_EN: On one hand I want to travel, but I am also afraid.
RELATED: på den anden side = on the other hand; derimod = by contrast; til gengæld = in return, on the other hand
## på forhånd
EXPL: "På forhånd" means "in advance". To thank someone before they do something, end your request with: "Kan du sende filen? Tak på forhånd!" (Can you send the file? Thanks in advance!) To say you paid ahead of time, say: "Jeg betalte på forhånd" (I paid in advance).
SENT_DA: Du skal booke bordet **på forhånd**.
SENT_EN: You must book the table in advance.
RELATED: i forvejen = beforehand; forud = ahead of time; tidligt = early; bagefter = afterwards
## på fuldtid
EXPL: "På fuldtid" means "full-time". It is built from "fuld" (full) and "tid" (time). To say someone is drunk, the word "fuld" is also used: "Han er fuld" (He is drunk).
SENT_DA: Hun arbejder **på fuldtid** og kommer hjem sent.
SENT_EN: She works full-time and comes home late.
RELATED: på deltid = part-time; overarbejde = overtime; en arbejdsuge = a work week; en arbejdstid = working hours
## på gensyn
EXPL: "På gensyn" means "see you again". It is built from "på" (on) and "et gensyn" (a seeing again). To end a visit politely, say: "Tak for i dag, på gensyn!" (Thanks for today, see you again!)
SENT_DA: Tak for besøget. **På gensyn!**
SENT_EN: Thanks for the visit. See you again!
RELATED: farvel = goodbye; hej hej = bye; hav det godt = take care; vi ses = see you
## på grund af
EXPL: "På grund af" means "because of". To give a reason that is a noun, say: "på grund af regnen" (because of the rain). To give a reason that has a verb in it, use "fordi" (because): "Jeg bliver hjemme, fordi det regner" (I am staying home because it is raining).
SENT_DA: Vi kom for sent **på grund af** trafikken.
SENT_EN: We were late because of the traffic.
RELATED: en årsag = a cause; derfor = therefore; takket være = thanks to; på trods af = despite
## på tide

SENT_EN: It is time to go to bed.
RELATED: i tide = in time; for sent = too late; højeste tid = high time; endelig = finally
