# Cards flagged under rule 9 (the card should be the general word)

Rule 9 in docs/LIGHTBULB_REVIEW.md: a free combination such as "en tid hos lægen" should be the general word ("en tid") with the rest as the example. These cards are only FLAGGED. Nothing in the card list changes without the owner's go-ahead.

Found by a script that looks for multi-word en/et cards whose first two words are already a card of their own. It cannot find free combinations whose general word is not a card yet, so a read of the Basic and Intermediate card list is still needed.

Free combinations (owner decides: replace by the general word, or keep):
- en tid hos lægen (a doctor's appointment) → "en tid" is already a card
- en følelse af lettelse (a sense of relief) → "en følelse" is already a card
- en flok fugle (a big flock of birds) → "en flok" is already a card

Fixed expressions that only look like free combinations (keep):
- en gang om ugen (once a week), en gang imellem (once in a while), et par jeans (a pair of jeans)
